# ha-team-forge

Factory skill + workflow for minting HA teams (plugins, agents, skills, contracts, workflows, ctl) in the exact ha-hackers shape.

Canonical at: `~/.grok/skills/ha-team-forge/`

This is the plugin mirror. Contains:
- commands/
- agents/forge-lead.md
- skills/ha-team-forge/ (synced copy)
- PLUGIN.md / README.md

Use `/ha-team-forge` or the workflow to forge new teams like ha-pumapay, ha-sentinel, ha-foo.
