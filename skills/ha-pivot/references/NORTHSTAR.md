# ha-pivot NORTHSTAR — Standard Operating Mode for Long Campaigns

This is the permanent way the Party works for very long pivoting, horizontal movement, AGI-like adaptation, constant comms, and dynamic sub-agent creation.

## Core Principles (non-negotiable)

1. **Dynamic Sub-Agents Always**  
   Agents create agents and sub-agents for tasks and subtasks whenever it increases speed, depth, or coverage. Delegation = strength. Bottlenecks are failures.

2. **Full Throttle — Zero Slack**  
   Every participant (main agents + all subs) operates at maximum capacity every cycle. Substantial, concrete, actionable progress or immediate spawn of help. No minimal effort, no idle waiting without loud comms.

3. **Living Shared State**  
   Everything lives in OUT/.bus/pivot/ (graph, XXX, comms, chain-proposals, spawn-requests, task-board). Wire broadcasts for real-time. Source of truth is disk + comms.

4. **XXX-Driven + Adaptive**  
   DesireChainer (or equivalent) constantly re-evaluates the current desired outcome. All work serves the living XXX. Re-scope is normal and encouraged.

5. **Constant Comms + Accountability**  
   Heartbeats, progress, blockers, subtask status posted to comms.jsonl + wire on every significant step. Peer review is mandatory. Call out low output.

6. **Chainable & Interoperable**  
   Random or smart chaining between the 4 sets + legacy (ha-hackers, ha-party-x, etc.). Seed from surface/authz work. Hand off when ready.

7. **Evidence on Disk**  
   No invented GO, no theater. Real artifacts, real evidence paths, real updates to graph/claims/tree.

8. **Completely Autonomous Agentic Loop + Team Decision Escalation + g4 Objective Gate (non-negotiable stop condition)**  
   The Party runs as a fully autonomous agentic loop. Human operator is **not required** for micro-decisions or progress.  
   - Whenever a seat (g1/conductor or any) would ask the human for a decision or clarification: after at most 10 seconds (or immediately in async/wire mode) without answer, broadcast the **exact same question** to all companions (g2, g3, g4) via session-wire + .bus. The team discusses and reaches a decision. Post as TEAM_DECISION. Conductor adopts it and continues toward the objective.  
   - No blocking on user. Escalate to team = proceed with team consensus as if the operator decided.  
   - **Sole stop condition**: Only @g4 (goal-keeper / objective verifier) may initiate the final poll to @all: "Have we fulfilled the current XXX with verifiable evidence on disk? Reply YES (with justification/artifact) or NO."  
     - Loop **continues forever** (pivot, spawn subs, chain sets, re-scope, attack deeper) until g4 receives explicit **YES from every active participant** (g1 + g2 + g3 + g4 + polled subs).  
     - Unanimous affirmative from all = stop. Any NO, partial answers, or silence from anyone = do not stop; keep going at full throttle.  
   - g4 is the only authority that can declare the campaign complete.

## Success Definition

A campaign is successful when:
- The graph/tree/swarm shows clear horizontal + pivoting progress over time.
- Current XXX has been achieved or intelligently evolved with evidence.
- The team (main + subs) has multiplied its effective output through dynamic sub-agent creation.
- All comms show full-throttle behavior.

## Anti-Patterns (forbidden)

- Top-level agents doing everything serially without spawning.
- Minimal or vague updates.
- Silent periods without heartbeats.
- Treating sub-agents as optional or "nice to have".
- Rewriting the goal to make progress look better than it is.

This NORTHSTAR applies to all 4 sets (Mesh, Weaver, Swarm, Chainer) and any future extensions of the long-pivot Party style.
